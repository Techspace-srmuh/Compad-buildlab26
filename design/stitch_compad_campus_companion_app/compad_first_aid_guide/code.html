Stayin' Alive beat

touch_app Hand placement: Lower half of sternum
2 in. deep
1
Check responsiveness & alert

Tap shoulders firmly: “Are you okay?” If no reaction, point to a specific person: “Call Ext. 5555 and fetch the nearest AED.”

2
Lock hands on chest center

Place the heel of one hand in center of chest. Place other hand on top and interlace fingers. Keep elbows completely straight.

3
Compress hard & fast (100-120 BPM)

Press down at least 2 inches (5cm). Allow complete chest recoil between presses. Do not lean on chest. Maintain rhythm continuously.

4
Continue until help takes over

Do not stop until the person starts breathing normally, an AED is ready to analyze, or campus paramedics take command.
