02. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM CONTEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Campus mobile connectivity is notoriously unstable—basement labs, dense lecture
halls, and overloaded campus Wi-Fi lead to constant dropouts. CompAd is a 100% 
offline-first, zero-latency mobile platform designed for ALL college students 
across any university or department.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
03. RECENT MAJOR UPDATES & NEW CAPABILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[✔] 1. UNIVERSAL STUDENT IDENTITY CHIP (REPLACED INSTITUTION-SPECIFIC VAULT)
    - Removed hardcoded institution cards (Central Library, Apex Institute, HPC).
    - Introduced the single, universal Student Identity Chip for ALL colleges.
    - Fields: Student Name, College Name, Roll No. / Reg No., Year of Study,
      and Department.
    - Setup once on install, with a prominent "Skip for now" option so students
      are never blocked or forced to fill out forms.
    - Details automatically link across the entire app (Hub greeting, ID chip,
      export headers, and sharing payloads).

[✔] 2. DYNAMIC QR CODE & DUAL P2P AIR-SHARE (BLUETOOTH + WI-FI)
    - Interactive QR code generated on the Student Identity Chip.
    - Tap to expand QR code for camera scanning by other CompAd users.
    - Two working transport options for sharing lightweight documents (timetables,
      notes, datesheets):
      * ⚡ Local Wi-Fi Direct: In-memory offline HTTP server with pairing QR code.
      * 📶 Bluetooth / Quick Share: Direct native wireless exchange with zero router.
    - Built-in camera scanner powered by mobile_scanner for 1-tap peer import.

[✔] 3. ANONYMOUS SHARING & FULL IDENTITY MODES
    - Privacy toggle: "Anonymous Identity" (hides name, college, and roll number)
      vs "Share Full Details" (attaches Student Identity Chip details).
    - Gives students full privacy control when transferring schedules or notes.

[✔] 4. LOCAL SHARING HISTORY & AUDIT TRAIL
    - Complete sent and received transfer history persisted in on-device SQLite.
    - Individual item deletion and "Clear All Records" option with confirmation.
    - 100% customizable and deletable by the user.

[✔] 5. BIOMETRIC SECURITY AS AN OPTIONAL SETTING
    - Biometrics are NO LONGER compulsory.
    - Managed via the new in-app "Settings & Security" screen.
    - Students can toggle Biometric App Lock ON or OFF anytime.
    - Smooth fallback and bypass options available.

[✔] 6. STUDENT-ENTERED TIMETABLE & ATTENDANCE + AI/ML IMAGE RECOGNITION
    - Timetable and attendance fully editable and created by students.
    - "AI Scan Image" feature: Student uploads a photo/screenshot of a timetable;
      on-device AI/ML text recognition (Google ML Kit OCR) extracts days,
      time ranges, course codes, and classrooms automatically.
    - Interactive review sheet previews courses and slots before 1-tap import.

[✔] 7. HUB & DASHBOARD BOTTOM ALIGNMENT FIX
    - Fixed bottom scrolling padding across Hub, Timetable, Lab, Notes, and Vault.
    - Floating action buttons and utility cards now sit with perfect clearance
      above the floating spatial glass dock without collision or cutoff.

[✔] 8. OPTIMIZED APP SIZE (< 100MB CONSTRAINT MET)
    - Standalone Mobile Release APK: ~45.2 MB (ARM64-v8a for modern Android phones).
    - 32-bit Legacy APK: ~35.8 MB.
    - Well within the 100MB limit with all AI models and database encryption included.
